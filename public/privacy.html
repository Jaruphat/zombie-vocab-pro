Privacy Policy for Zombie Vocab Pro

Last updated: April 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zombie Vocab Pro ("the App") handles information when you use our mobile application and website.

1. Data Collection

Zombie Vocab Pro is designed to be a standalone educational game. We do not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell your personal data.

All game progress, custom vocabulary words, and user settings are stored locally on your device (using standard local storage/IndexedDB mechanisms). This data never leaves your device and is not synchronized to any external servers by us.

2. Internet Permissions

The App may require basic internet permissions strictly for the purpose of serving essential web assets if played in a browser, or for standard Webview functionality on mobile platforms. We do not use the internet to track your behavior or collect telemetry.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or analytic services that collect user data.

4. Children's Privacy

Because our App does not collect any personal information, it is safe for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please do not hesitate to contact the developer directly via the Google Play Store contact information provided on our app listing page.
